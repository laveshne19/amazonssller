## Product description

Plain text, used if A+ Content is not active on this ASIN.

```
Most of what gets said in a meeting is gone by the time you get back to your desk. Recolx fixes that.

Recolx Tap is an AI voice recorder about the size of a credit card. Clip it to the back of your phone or drop it in your pocket, press record, and it captures the whole conversation. The Recolx app turns that audio into a transcript, a summary, key takeaways and a to do list, in 112 languages.

Who it's for
Students recording lectures. Sales reps who need call notes in the CRM. Doctors, lawyers and consultants who would rather listen than type. Journalists doing interviews. Anyone who has ever said "wait, what did we agree on?"

What you get
- In person mode for meetings, classes and interviews
- Call mode that records both sides of a phone call through the back of the phone
- Up to 30 hours of continuous recording on a single charge
- 64GB onboard storage
- Bluetooth sync to the Recolx app
- Summaries, mind maps, AI chat and ready made templates
- Export to PDF, Word, Markdown, Notion, Google Docs and Slack
- 3 months of the Pro plan included

Good to know
Call recording laws differ by state and country. Please let the other person know you are recording. Phone audio cannot be captured while you are on a Bluetooth headset, so use the phone speaker or earpiece for calls you want to record.

The last paragraph is deliberate. It answers the two most common reasons people return call recorders, and it protects the listing from compliance complaints.

## Backend search terms

```
dictaphone audio recording device transcriber notetaker digital dictation memo small mini portable wearable magsafe iphone android class student interview journalist doctor nurse sales speech to text translate mind map minutes

Byte count: 226.

Words I left out on purpose:
- Competitor names like Plaud, Otter or Sony. Amazon prohibits them in search terms and it can get the ASIN suppressed.
- "Best", "cheap", "sale", "new". Not allowed and ignored by the A9 index anyway.
- Anything already in the title. Amazon indexes title words, so repeating them here wastes bytes.
